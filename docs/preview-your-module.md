# Preview Your Module

The Module Preview shows your module the way learners will see it in the
course, page by page, with what to fix in the document beside each page. It
works from a Word copy of your Google Doc. Nothing is built or imported, and
you do not need Studio.

**[Open the Module Preview](https://github.mit.edu/pages/jmartis/wordthang-converter/)** (opens in a new tab)

## Your document stays on your computer

The preview reads your document in your own browser. It is not uploaded
anywhere, and nobody else can see it. If you add a video list, the videos
play from the video platform, as any web video does; your document and the
list stay in your browser.

## Start from your Google Doc

Your module is a Google Doc, and the preview reads Word documents. To preview
it, download a copy as a Word document:

1. In your Google Doc, choose **File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx)**.
2. Open the downloaded file in the Module Preview, as described below.

Make your fixes in the Google Doc, not in the downloaded copy. The copy is
only for previewing: changes made to it do not reach the Google Doc.

The preview does not see changes you make in the Google Doc. After a round
of changes, download it again, then select **Choose another…** (in browsers
other than Chrome and Edge, **Choose it again…**) and pick the new download.
**Read it again** only rereads the copy you already opened.

## Open your document

1. Open the Module Preview. The first visit loads about 12 MB, so "Getting
   the preview ready…" shows for a few seconds. Later visits are faster.
2. Select **Choose your document…** and pick your module's Word document, or
   drag the document onto the page. The preview reads it, which takes a few
   seconds for a large module, and shows its name and number of pages.
3. If you have the course's video list, add it now (see
   [Play your videos](#play-your-videos)). You can also drop the document
   and the list onto the page together.
4. Select **Preview your module**. The first page of your module appears.

The preview reads Word documents (.docx). If your document is a .doc, is
saved as a Strict Open XML Document, or is protected by a password, open it
in Word and save it as a **Word Document (.docx)** with no password.

## See it as learners do

Switch between two looks with **Simple** and **edX** at the top of the page.

- **Simple** shows the course outline on the left, the page in the middle,
  and **To fix in the document** on the right.
- **edX** is laid out like the course's own learning page: the breadcrumb at
  the top (select a section or subsection name to jump to another), the row
  of pages in the subsection, and **Previous** and **Next**. Select
  **Course** in the breadcrumb to see the course outline, and **Resume** to
  return to the page you were on.

Move between pages with **Previous** and **Next**, or select a page in the
outline. The preview remembers which page you were on, and returns you there
the next time you open the same document.

Problems show their questions, choices, Submit button, and attempts as
learners see them, but you cannot answer them in the preview. Select
**Preview the answer** under a problem to see the answer and explanation
that learners see once they finish it. Videos show where they will play,
and say when a video still needs its ID set in Studio. To watch them in
place, add your video list: see [Play your videos](#play-your-videos).

## Play your videos

Add the video platform's list of the course's videos, the same CSV used to
build the course (with the columns Name, Video ID, Status, hls URL and
desktop_mp4 URL), and each video plays in its place on the page.

1. Select **Add your video list (CSV)…** on the start page, before or after
   choosing your document, or **Add video list…** at the top of the page
   once your module is shown. You
   can also drop the CSV onto the page, with the document or on its own.
2. Each video your document names is matched to the list by its file name,
   the same way the course build matches it. A video found plays with the
   usual controls.
3. The top of the page shows how many of your document's videos were found,
   for example "2 of 4 found". Each video that was not found is listed under
   **Videos to set up** for its page, with the reason: no video in the list
   has that name, its upload has not finished, or the list has only an
   earlier version of it.

The preview keeps the list for your next visit. Select **Remove** to stop
using it. If the platform's list changes, add the new one. In Chrome or
Edge, a new export saved over the same file is read again by itself.

Transcripts still do not show: they are uploaded in Studio. If a video does
not play, open its desktop_mp4 address from the list in a browser tab; the
address has to open without signing in to edX.

## Fix what it finds

**To fix in the document** lists what to change in your Word document for
the page shown, such as a picture with no alt text or a problem missing its
explanation. Each item says what is wrong, and **How to fix it** opens the
guide that covers it. Under the list, **elsewhere in the module** collects
the items for other pages, each with a button that takes you to its page.
The number beside a page in the outline is how many items that page has.

## Edit and save as you go

This is for a document you save in Word. For a Google Doc, see
[Start from your Google Doc](#start-from-your-google-doc).

In **Chrome** or **Edge**, keep the preview open while you work. Each time
you save in Word, the preview reads your document again and shows the
change, usually within a few seconds, on the page you were looking at. The
bar at the top says when the document was last read.

- If Word is still saving when the preview reads the document, the preview
  keeps showing the version before your save and tries again. If it still
  cannot read the save, it says so above the page.
- **Read it again** reads the document straight away.
- **Choose another…** opens a different document.
- The next time you open the preview, **Preview *your document* again**
  opens the same document. Chrome or Edge may ask you to allow the preview to
  read it.

In other browsers, the preview does not see your saves. After you save in
Word, select **Choose it again…** and pick the document again.

## Start over

Select **Start over** at the top of the page to clear everything: the
document shown (the preview stops following its saves), the video list,
the document offered again on your next visit, and the page you were on in
each document. You return to the start page, as on a first visit. Your
choice of **Simple** or **edX** is kept.
